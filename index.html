01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I’m Gauresh Nitin Bhatia — a first-year student studying CSE with a specialization in Cyber Security at Vellore Institute of Technology.

I chose this field because I am deeply interested in how technology can protect people—not just systems. In a world where so much of daily life depends on digital platforms, individual privacy, personal data, and online safety have become fundamental needs. I want to understand how attackers exploit weaknesses and, more importantly, how ethical security practices can prevent that harm. At VIT, I’m building a foundation in SOC (Security Operations Centre) and Quantum Cryptography while learning to ask better questions, work with care, and turn my learnings into something practical.

This portfolio is a snapshot of that process. You’ll find a project in progress, three courses that shaped how I think, writing samples, and a concise résumé. It’s a record of what I’ve learned so far — and an invitation to see what I might explore next.
