ENGLISH POEM

The Crimson
Castle

WRITTEN BY
GAURESH NITIN BHATIA

A crimson castle, once so grand,
Stood tall and proud upon the land.
Its noble king, of heart so true,
Ruled wisely, loved by all he knew.

But darkness crept, a wicked hand,
To steal his kingdom, claim his land.
With treachery and guile, he sought
To seize the throne, for which he fought.

The king, condemned, a cruel decree,
Was burned alive, a horrifying scene.
His ashes scattered, lost to time,
A noble soul, a tragic crime.

The crimson castle, once so bright,
Now stands in ruins, shrouded in night.
A symbol of a fallen king,
A tale of woe, a mournful thing.

MATHEMATICAL MODELING

SIR Model for
Malware Propagation

BY
GAURESH NITIN BHATIA

PROJECT / MATHEMATICAL SIMULATION

Modeling malware spread with SIR.

This project applies the Susceptible–Infected–Removed (SIR) framework to malware propagation in a computer network. Systems move from vulnerable (S) to infected (I), then to removed (R) through cleanup, patching, or isolation.

dS/dt = −βSI
dI/dt = βSI − γI
dR/dt = γI

The basic reproduction number, R0 = βN/γ, describes the outbreak threshold: values above 1 indicate growth, while values below 1 indicate decline. The infection peak occurs at S = γ/β.

I simulated the system in GNU Octave with ode45, plotting S(t), I(t), and R(t), as well as an S–I phase plane to examine outbreak dynamics and long-term behavior.

Skills: Differential equations, mathematical modeling, dynamical systems, numerical simulation, GNU Octave, and data visualization.
