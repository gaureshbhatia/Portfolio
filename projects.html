A project is a way to turn a question into something you can test. Here’s one I’m currently exploring.

ONGOING EXPLORATION / 2025–26

Campus energy,
made visible.

PROJECT 01
01

The question

How might students and staff better understand the energy used in everyday campus spaces? This sample project imagines an approachable dashboard that makes consumption patterns easier to notice and discuss.

02

The approach

I’m beginning with background research and short interviews to understand what information people actually find useful. Next, I’ll map a typical user journey, sketch low-fidelity screens, and build a small prototype using sample data. Feedback sessions will guide each iteration.

03

Expected outcome

The goal is a tested interface concept that communicates energy use clearly and suggests practical next steps. I expect the prototype and feedback notes to show which visualisations help people compare usage and where the experience needs more explanation.
