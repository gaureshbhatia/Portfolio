LEARNING AT VIT / THREE COURSES

What I’ve been learning.

Three courses that helped me connect core ideas to practical skills — and changed how I approach a problem.

01

CYBERSECURITY / VIT, VELLORE

Post Quantum Era Cryptography

This course explores how quantum computing could change the foundations of digital security. It connects the risks to today’s public-key cryptography with the need to design systems that can remain secure in a post-quantum world.

SUBJECT KNOWLEDGE

Quantum threats to current cryptography and the goals of post-quantum cryptographic methods.

SKILLS DEVELOPED

Assessing cryptographic assumptions and thinking about long-term security and migration.

02

QUANTUM MECHANICS / VIT, VELLORE

Engineering Physics

A grounding in physics at the quantum scale, where familiar classical ideas give way to probability and discrete states. The course helps make sense of the principles behind modern technologies such as semiconductors and lasers.

SUBJECT KNOWLEDGE

Wave–particle duality, quantum states, and the mathematical description of quantum systems.

SKILLS DEVELOPED

Applying physical models and equations to interpret unfamiliar phenomena.

↗
03

MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS / VIT, VELLORE

Multivariable Calculus and Differential Equations

This course extends calculus to functions of several variables and to systems that change over time. These tools provide a mathematical language for modelling and solving problems across engineering and the sciences.

SUBJECT KNOWLEDGE

Partial derivatives, multiple integrals, vector calculus, and ordinary differential equations.

SKILLS DEVELOPED

Choosing suitable methods to analyse functions, rates of change, and mathematical models.
